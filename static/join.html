Questions

What's in a property report?

Roof area, squares and waste; ridges, hips, valleys, rakes, eaves and flashing; pitch and facet diagrams; wall area by side with elevations, gutters, fascia, soffit and an estimated window count; and the lot and yard: lawn (grass only), driveway, sidewalk, beds and lot size. On the web and as a PDF.

Is "unlimited" really unlimited?

Yes, for single-property reports your company orders for its own estimating. ZIP code lists are bought separately, at half price for members.

How do I cancel?

Log in and open "Billing" to update your card or cancel. Your membership runs to the end of the month you paid for.

How accurate is it?

Measured from county aerial photos and LiDAR. Roof area is typically within a few percent of professional reports. Treat it as an estimate and verify on site before ordering material or final billing.

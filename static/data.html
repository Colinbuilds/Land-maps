Good to know

Does it replace a site visit?

It replaces the measuring, not your judgement. Measurements are estimates from aerial photos and LiDAR; confirm on the first visit before final billing. Every row has a quality flag so you know which ones to double-check.

What's in the free sample?

25 real properties picked from across the ZIP, with every column the full sheet has. One free sample per email.

Can I pick any ZIP?

Any ZIP in Douglas, Sarpy, Lancaster and Cass counties, Nebraska today, with more counties coming.

Is owner information included?

No. Sheets contain addresses and measurements, not owner names or mailing addresses, so they're simple to use for postcards addressed to "Resident".

Measurements come from public aerial photos, LiDAR and county parcel data, which can be a few years old. Sheets contain no owner names or mailing addresses. Coverage today: Douglas, Sarpy, Lancaster and Cass counties, Nebraska.

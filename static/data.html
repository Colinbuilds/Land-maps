Questions

Does it replace a site visit?

It replaces the measuring, not your judgement. Measurements are estimates from aerial photos and LiDAR; confirm on the first visit before final billing. Every row has a quality flag so you know which to double-check.

How accurate is it?

Lawn is traced on recent aerial photos, with LiDAR removing anything raised (decks, sheds, play sets, shrubs) and infrared confirming what's actually growing. Check the free sample against yards you've already measured.

What's in the free sample?

25 real properties picked from across the ZIP, with every column the full sheet has. One free sample per email.

Is owner information included?

No. Sheets have addresses and measurements, not owner names or mailing addresses, so they're simple to use for postcards to "Resident".

Which ZIPs can I pick?

Any ZIP in Douglas, Sarpy, Lancaster and Cass counties, Nebraska today, with more counties coming.

How do I pay and download?

Pay by card at checkout. You get a private order page that updates on its own and has the download button when the sheet is ready.

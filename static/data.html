Lawn: priced before you knock

7,480 sq ft×$4.00 / 1,000=$30

7,480 sq ft of lawn × your $4.00 per 1,000 sq ft = $30 per fertilizer application, or $150 for a 5-step season. Drop the column into a spreadsheet formula and every home on the list has its own price.

How we measure

What the numbers come from, and when to double-check.

Aerial photos, LiDAR height data and county records. Homes that are hard to read are flagged. See how
Aerial photos

Lawn, driveway, sidewalk and patios are traced on the county's recent aerial photos (or USDA aerial photos where the county has none). Each area shows its photo source and year when you pick it.

LiDAR height data

Laser height scans take out anything raised (decks, sheds, play sets, shrubs) so the lawn is grass only, and measure house walls.

County records

Lot lines, address, owner, year built and assessed value come from the county's public parcel data.

Flagged for review

Homes where the photo is hard to read, like heavy tree cover or most of the yard looking paved, are marked "review" in the spreadsheet so you know which to check.

We haven't published accuracy figures yet: we'll only show error rates once they're checked against on-site measurements. Until then, the free preview is the best check. Include up to 5 homes you've already measured yourself.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Does it replace a site visit?

It replaces the measuring, not your judgement. Measurements are estimates from aerial photos and LiDAR; confirm on the first visit before final billing. Every row has a quality flag so you know which to double-check.

How accurate is it?

Lawn is traced on recent aerial photos, with LiDAR removing anything raised (decks, sheds, play sets, shrubs) and infrared confirming what's actually growing. We haven't published accuracy figures yet (see "How we measure" above). The free preview lets you check homes you've already measured.

Can I buy just a few streets?

Yes. Choose Draw an area, click around the neighborhood, subdivision or streets you want, and you'll see the exact homes and price. Areas can cross ZIP lines; every home is counted once. Whole ZIPs are still available.

How does job size work?

Pick one measurement and a minimum, a maximum or both, like "Driveway: 1,000 sq ft and up" or "Lawn: 4,000–10,000 sq ft". Homes exactly at a limit are included; homes without that measurement are left out. That measurement decides which homes you get; any other measurements you tick come along as extra columns.

How is the price worked out?

Per home: 10¢ for the first measurement and 5¢ for each extra one (Premier: 25¢ a home). Homes in higher assessed-value bands cost a little more. Every order has a $25 minimum; members then pay half. You see the exact total, and the line items, before checkout, and that's what you pay.

What's a Premier list?

The full property report for every home on your list, as one PDF per home in a folder: roof lengths, pitch and facets, walls and elevations, gutters, and the lot and yard on the aerial photo. It's for roofing, gutter, siding and window companies; lawn and snow companies usually just need the spreadsheet.

What's in the free preview?

Up to 15 homes with every column the paid spreadsheet has. Up to 5 are addresses you already service in the area you picked (any size), so you can check our measurements against properties you know. Up to 10 more come from the homes that match your area and job size, from small to large. Add your rate to see your price on each home and on a sample postcard. One free preview per email.

Is owner information included?

Yes: the owner's name and mailing address from public county records, where the state allows it for marketing (not in Kansas). Follow do-not-mail and do-not-call requests.

Which ZIPs can I pick?

Omaha and Lincoln today, with Des Moines, Kansas City, Denver and more metros being added every week. Type your ZIP above to see if it's ready.

What does membership add?

Half off every ZIP sheet, drawn-area list and Premier folder, unlimited property reports for your trades, postcard files for any home, and the CRM. When a member buys a list, every home goes straight into their CRM as a customer with an estimate already priced from their rates. Cancel any time.

How do I pay and download?

Pay by card at checkout. You get a private order page that updates on its own and has the download button when the sheet is ready.

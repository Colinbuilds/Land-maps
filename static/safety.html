Safety

What's protecting your site and data, what still needs setting up, and a record of every owner action.

Set-up checklist

Backups

Every database and setting, copied every night; the newest 14 are kept on the server. Download one every week or so and keep it off the server (your computer or Google Drive).

Always on

  • Wrong passwords or admin keys: 10 tries in 15 minutes locks that connection out for 15 minutes.
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never as text. Companies can reset theirs by email.
  • Sign-in cookies are signed, secure (HTTPS only) and can't be read by scripts.
  • Browsers must use HTTPS, and other sites can't frame your pages.
  • Each company sees only its own reports, customers and money. Reports only carry its trades' sections.
  • Paused companies can't sign in. Every owner change is logged below.
